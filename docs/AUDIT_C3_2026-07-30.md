## 4. Separate the plant-dependent from the plant-independent

This distinction should govern what gets acted on.

**Plant-dependent (conditional on a fiat family):** 1,711/2,385 saturating,
14 macro-cycle limit cycles, 6 stationary, the per-gain distribution. Real, but
contingent on a plant nobody has validated against athletes.

**Plant-independent (structural):**

1. The 21-day observer window spans block days 7–27, so its *old* trend
   sub-window holds corrected loading weeks and its *recent* sub-window holds the
   **uncorrected week-4 deload**. An improving trajectory therefore reads as
   continued headroom. That is a property of the observer and the block template,
   not of the plant.
2. A healthy athlete has **no gate on raises**. The monotone-conservative
   override requires a caution class, restrictive guardrail, or injured joint
   (`kinematicAutopilot.ts:395-400`). `deriveDailyAdjustment` has zero callers
   (`DEVIATION_LOG.md:96-105`), so daily readiness modulates nothing. The only
   stop is `base_rpe_cap`.
3. The RPE channel is unrationed. §7 quantifies it: a cut reaches all six
   non-deload occurrences (`set Δ = −6`), a raise grants `+1` at most and is
   often denied by the two-set budget — but `±0.5` RPE reaches every non-deload
   occurrence in both directions.

**§7's paired healthy/niggle A/B is the cleanest experiment in the report.**
Same plant, same seed, same archetype: the knee niggle at severity 4 blocks all
eight raises; the healthy athlete receives all eight. That isolates the gate and
does not depend on plant realism. It confirms the C2 audit's §4d concern exactly.

**The structural findings justify action. The statistics only size it.**

## 5. Where I would push

- **Classification thresholds are arbitrary and the counts are sensitive to
  them.** `limit_cycle` = last four actions nonzero and alternating;
  `saturated_*` = last three one-directional with a nonlinear bind. Defensible,
  but "14" and "6" would move under different windows. The qualitative result —
  deterministic zero-noise counterexamples exist, and the nominal case saturates
  upward — does not move. State that when quoting numbers.
- **584 `mixed` cases (24.5%) are unresolved at 8 blocks.** Not a defect; just
  unclaimed. A longer horizon would settle them.
- **Exact onset below `RPE_GAIN = 0.25` is open.** Sol says so (§4).
- **Is the deload straddle universal or template-specific?** §8 shows a week-1
  deload converts continuous raising into raise/neutral alternation. If real
  macro phases predominantly deload in week 4, the bias is systematic and
  option 4 is well-targeted. If placement varies by phase, the remediation must
  handle both. Worth establishing before designing the fix.

## 6. On the proposal

The five options are fairly stated and option 5 (accept) is correctly ruled out
on present evidence. Two additions to Sol's recommended 4+2+3 combination:

**Sequence rather than combine.** Landing three changes at once makes it
impossible to attribute which one fixed what, against a counterexample set this
specific.

**Option 3 is the cheap immediate bound; option 4 is the cure.** A cumulative
per-macro-cycle RPE budget — the upward analogue of `MAX_ADDED_SETS` — does not
touch the observer, bounds the demonstrated harm directly, and can precede the
observer work.

**Option 4 has a larger blast radius than it appears.** Changing the trend
reference changes φ, which invalidates the analytic pins in
`verify_autopilot.mjs` — the gate that protects the autopilot would have to be
rewritten in the same change as the autopilot itself. That is a combination
worth isolating and sequencing deliberately, not absorbing into a combined fix.

## 7. Recommended C4 shape

Sol offers two. I recommend the **first now, second later**: land a gate that
pins the six stationary counterexamples and the nominal upward-saturation case
as **expected-failure evidence**. That machine-encodes the finding so it cannot
regress silently, gives any future remediation a regression target, and ships
without touching the controller.

Also warranted at ratification: a `DEVIATION_LOG.md` entry, since this is a known
defect in shipped behaviour, and a `RELEASE_READINESS.md` note — §D of that
document already treats prescription-derived liability exposure.
