## 1. Three things done right

**1a. 3.0 was rejected under a rule declared before the data.** It had zero limit
cycles and zero upward saturation. It would have been easy to argue "no cycles,
no saturation, ship it." Instead the predeclared early-warning rule — `mixed`
drifting toward the unbounded control — was honoured: 401 → 463 against
unbounded's 569. That is the rule working as designed, and honouring it when it
costs you the preferred answer is the whole point of declaring it in advance.

**1c. The classifier bug was found by going where the metric could see.** The
C6A audit flagged that trajectory labels inspect blocks 6–8 while grants fill
forward from block 1, so no ceiling below 3.0 could move the label. Testing 3.0
surfaced that the historical classifier was additionally **one boundary late**
and could not observe the block-6 grant at all. The flag led directly to the
discovery.

**1b. The rationale comment does not launder the outcome.**
`kinematicAutopilot.ts:78-87` records, in order: the corrective-overlay
reasoning; that **Francis preferred six blocks / ~24 weeks**; that C6B **rejected
3.0** and why; that 2.5 keeps **five blocks / ~20 weeks**; and that unbounded
remains a NO-GO.

This is the note I was going to require and it was already there. A future reader
learns that someone wanted six blocks, the evidence said no, and five is what
shipped. The stated justification matches what the code does — which is exactly
the property whose absence this entire chain has been correcting.

## 4. Follow-up — restate the historical figures under the corrected classifier

Two classification views are now in circulation with different numbers: `mixed`
389 (historical) versus 401 (applied) at baseline, and 562 versus 569 unbounded.
Every prior document — `SWEEP_C3.md`, the C5 and C6 remediations, C6A — used the
historical view, computed under a classifier now known to be one boundary late.

For ceilings ≤ 2.5 this is inert: grants never reach the label window, so the
off-by-one cannot bite. **C3 is the exception.** It ran pre-R1, when the RPE
channel was unrationed and every block could raise, so the label window
definitely contained granted actions. Its headline counts — 1,505 upward
saturations, 1,711 saturating, 14 limit cycles — could move under the corrected
view.

The *findings* do not depend on this. C3's counterexamples are demonstrated by
explicit per-block action tables and are pinned individually in the
counterexample gate; a labelling bug cannot touch either. But those aggregate
figures are quoted in `DEVIATION_LOG.md` and `RELEASE_READINESS.md`.

Required: re-run the C3 baseline under the corrected classifier and either
confirm the headline figures or restate them where they are quoted. Small, and
this project has not been in the habit of accepting "probably unchanged."
