# WO-04 persona and current-policy audit

Baseline `87624d9e43189ddd87db317e24d4379ef5a13fae`, inspected and tested 2026-09-12. This is a source audit with existing automated gates, not observation of a device or a newly implemented weekly planner. Proposed scenarios below are acceptance specifications; they have not been implemented as new tests in this documentation-only work.

## Findings requiring disposition

1. Experience alone currently increases dose in two planners. [Block generator](../../../packages/inference/src/blockGenerator.ts#L886) adds an elite set; [routine defaults](../../../packages/inference/src/routineMicrocycle.ts#L200) add sets for advanced/elite, and [routine budgets](../../../packages/inference/src/routineMicrocycle.ts#L206) allow greater stress by tier. Existing [block gate](../../../packages/inference/test/verify_blocks.mjs#L205) asserts lower beginner volume. This conflicts with the new WO-04 requirement, even though those gates pass. The owner must ratify a replacement or an explicitly restricted rollout before claiming conformance.
2. A new profile's large assumed week and symptom logic are product choices, not validated first-week/medical rules. [Defaults](../../../packages/inference/src/types.ts#L358) assume intermediate experience and a full schedule. [Symptom scaling](../../../packages/inference/src/profileLimits.ts#L80) explicitly assumes beginner complaints are more likely benign DOMS. No source in this review validates that diagnostic shortcut. Existing tests assert it; removing it needs review and changed tests, not silent reuse for disabilities.
3. The current weekly planning contract does not express the requested activity/clinical context. [UserProfile](../../../packages/inference/src/types.ts#L341), [BlockInput](../../../packages/inference/src/blockGenerator.ts#L205), [scheduled session](../../../packages/inference/src/blockGenerator.ts#L163), and [routine input](../../../packages/inference/src/routineMicrocycle.ts#L65) use profile, day and movement-oriented fields. A date arithmetic helper does not establish timezone/DST support for recurring local events. The existing `hybrid` split specifically inserts BJJ; it cannot be repurposed as arbitrary existing sport scheduling.
4. Time and workload units cannot be merged indiscriminately. Slot-count budgeting is not an elapsed-time guarantee; routine family stress is not session-duration load, and neither is a clinical safety score. Existing logged strength days do not automatically represent swimming, walking or custom sport.
