## 1. Policy Boundary During Closed Beta

Under `D10` and `SC-08`, symptom-handling rules do not become less conservative before a qualified clinical reviewer signs off in writing. Closed-beta testing proceeds under the current fail-closed symptom rules while the items below are reviewed. No code loosening any symptom threshold, restriction, or negation rule may land before written sign-off is recorded here.
