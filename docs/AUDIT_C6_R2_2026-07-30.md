## 5. Precision notes

**5a. The phase partition is window-relative, not prescription-relative.**
`floor(i/7)` segments the 21-day window, and those segments coincide with
prescription weeks only when the window offset is `≡ 0 mod 7`. §4 shows offsets
0, −3 and −7 all converging, and −3 reproduces offset 0 exactly, so the practical
impact looks nil. But the stated invariant — *"compares error movement only
within the same prescription phase"* — is stronger than the implementation
guarantees at non-aligned offsets. Either weaken the claim in the doc, or align
the partition to the prescription week rather than the window index.

**5b. `last(V_q) − first(V_q)` discards the interior.** With two observations it
is the only available estimate. With six it throws away four and inherits full
sensitivity to the two endpoints. Defensible for auditability — every operation
stays hand-checkable, which matches this codebase's posture — but note it as a
known noise characteristic rather than an accident.

**5c. Two residual populations are unexplained**, neither alarming: 3
`saturated_down` (0.1%) and `mixed` rising 246 → 389. Downward is the safe
direction and cuts are unrationed by design, so 3 is plausibly genuine overreach
being handled correctly — but characterise them rather than leaving them silent.
