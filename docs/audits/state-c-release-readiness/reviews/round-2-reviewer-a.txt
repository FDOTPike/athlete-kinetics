## 3. Adjudication of OW-001's BLOCKING status

BLOCKING is supportable. I affirm it.

I re-derived the whole causal chain from primary sources rather than accepting the addendum's:

1. L1(a) is a ratified owner ruling requiring "Ambiguous mixed movements require athlete selection"
   — RELEASE_CANDIDATE_C1_DOCKET.md@a80f955:452.
2. At 34f91ff, isPurelyBodyweight is m.plannedImplement === 'Bodyweight' (blockGenerator.ts:113-114),
   and plannedImplementFor leaves every multi-prefix movement undeclared, so an ambiguous slot is
   never bodyweight.
3. That one flag gates both the Option C set ramp (:846, :853-858) and the rep floor (:862,
   slotReps = bodyweightSlot ? bodyweightRepsFor(m) : reps). One predicate, two behaviours, plus
   the fatigue-class accessor at :681-682.
4. At 48719b0 the predicate was m.primaryImplement === 'Bodyweight' (blockGenerator.ts:100-101),
   and the docket measured Push-up among the seven movements listing Bodyweight first (:254-257).
   So Push-up WAS bodyweight at State A and received both the bodyweight set delta and
   Math.max(reps, DEFAULT_ADVANCEMENT_POLICY.requiredReps) (State A :736).
5. Push-up is ('Push-up','handstand-push-up',0) — rung 0 of a seeded chain
   (016_movement_library_seed.sql:281), whose bar is requiredReps: 8 (progressionEngine.ts:51-54).

The decisive point, which I checked because the obvious rebuttal is the docket's "progression_policy
has zero seeded rows, so no athlete is affected today": that mitigation does not reach the floor.
bodyweightRepsFor at 34f91ff:770 falls back to DEFAULT_ADVANCEMENT_POLICY.requiredReps when no
policy row exists. The bar of 8 is therefore live now for all 15 chain movements. The regression is
current, not hypothetical.

So the harm that carries the status is NOT the load-class routing — L1(a) expressly blesses failing
closed to loaded for missing state. It is the loss of the capability-ladder rep floor for a movement
that is on a chain, which no ruling authorized. L2(b) narrowed the floor to chain movements; it did
not remove it from them. This reintroduces the exact defect section 6 of
AUDIT_architecture_review_8b4e75b.md identified, against the owner's verbatim instruction to
reconcile the ladder "so athletes can actually level up to harder variations" (docket:302-304). A
release candidate that regresses an owner-directed fix, on a shipped progression path, without
ratification, is the paradigm case for BLOCKING.

Two constraints on that affirmation:
- It must stay labelled what it is — the executor's conservative status inference, not an owner
  ruling. Section 4's preamble and addendum 1.1/4.2 do label it correctly, and the mandate's
  ratification firewall is not breached by populating a required schema column.
- Marking it NON_BLOCKING would be equally an executor judgment, and the less conservative one.
  There is no neutral option here; given a live, unratified dose change, conservatism is the
  correct default.

Per the addendum's own observation, either answer leaves release posture unchanged — C6 is
independently deferred.

Caveat carried forward as finding F: the row should be split. The athlete-selection residue alone
would be NON_BLOCKING; the predicate-fan-out regression is what is BLOCKING. Fusing them means
closing the blocking half cannot be recorded without closing the other.
