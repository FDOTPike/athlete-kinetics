## Decision and evidence boundary

Recommend a first release that records optional functional preferences and private notes, labels clinician instructions as user-reported transcriptions, and can hold explicitly affected coach recommendations. It must not interpret prose, certify clearance, choose a treatment plan, or advertise medical adaptation. A visible claim that recommendations are held is permitted only after all affected entry points below actually enforce the hold. Merely persisting a `held` field is insufficient.

The shared docket is explicitly proposed, reserves 064 and authorizes no schema/product behavior (`docs/decisions/ACCESSIBLE_COACH_SHARED_CONTRACT_DOCKET.md:3-8`). This review supplies a concrete proposal, not its ratification. WO-04 separates activity capture from prescribing and assigns persistence ownership to integration (`docs/research/accessible-coach/WO04_POLICY_DECISION_DOCKET.md:3-5`). Its clinical evidence entries are review inputs, not a qualified clinical sign-off (`docs/research/accessible-coach/WO04_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md:77-104`). No external source was fetched or independently clinically appraised in this bounded offline review.

Preserve offline operation, explicit deterministic inputs, strict TypeScript, STRICT SQLite and append-only migrations. The actual memory contract is a 450,000,000-byte preferred target and 536,870,912-byte ceiling (`AGENT_WORKFLOW.md:32-43`); the work-order's shorthand is not a new ceiling. This documentation changes neither.

Options considered: (A) capture plus an explicit product hold, recommended because it is usable without inventing medical semantics; (B) a new numerical restriction engine, deferred because unit/operator/applicability/stop transitions need clinical review; (C) automatic interpretation of existing injury text, rejected because it would invent restrictions and provenance. A costs an extra explicit user choice about whether instructions need review and limits the first slice; B/C cannot be made clinically validated by software tests alone.

## Minimal Health and Training Support slice

Optional fields: a preferred position (seated, recumbent/reclined, standing, no preference, other, unanswered), a user-reported concern about position changes, rest preference/needs as text, symptom-trigger notes, general support notes and clinician-instruction text. No diagnosis is required. Do not infer a disability from equipment, a condition from symptoms, a movement's position from its name, or suitability from matching a preference. No dose, symptom threshold, hydration/salt quantity, POTS escalation, HR ceiling or screening questionnaire is selected by this review.

Every form says: “Saved as your note. The app does not interpret this text or verify medical clearance.” Clinician notes say “Clinician instruction, entered by you — not independently verified.” Functional preferences say “Preference recorded; exercise suitability has not been assessed.” A separate explicit user action can request a pause of coach suggestions or identify instructions needing review. Text content never triggers that state. A clinician-instruction record is an explicitly selected record type, not a keyword classification.

Recommended owner policy for that action: record `review_required`, hold the explicitly affected prescription scope, and explain “Coach suggestions for this activity are on hold while your instructions are reviewed. Your history and notes remain available.” An unresolved applicability target holds all personalized prescription for that athlete; it does not block the entire app. The first slice has no route to `cleared_with_restrictions`, and confirmation only confirms transcription. A plain note or position preference by itself never causes disability-based exclusion or represents absence of restrictions.

Display/storage alone can ship only with honest “review requested/not assessed” copy. To ship actual held recommendations, implement the product-boundary evaluator and all relevant wrappers together. This is a software routing decision Francis can ratify; it is not clinical screening or proof that continuing any exercise is medically appropriate. A user cannot clear an instruction review by tapping “confirmed.” Product-only self-requested pauses may be withdrawn explicitly; unresolved clinical instructions remain review-required until the future reviewed workflow exists.

Privacy/lifecycle recommendation: keep records inside the existing athlete database, collapsed by default, reveal by an explicit action, do not put notes in list previews, diagnostics, error messages, notifications or analytics. Existing athlete databases are separate files; Coach Mode is not authentication (`apps/mobile/src/state/athleteRegistryCore.ts:4-18`; `apps/mobile/src/state/athleteRegistryCore.ts:40-42`). Hiding text is not access control or encryption. An app lock/role-based privacy model is a Francis/platform decision, not implicitly supplied here.

Editing an instruction creates a new revision and requires new transcription confirmation; older advice becomes stale before another work step. No revision changes completed session dose, set records or old schedule facts. Delete-note deletes its own text/revisions, not a separate instruction. Delete-instruction removes all its sensitive revisions/applicability and invalidates derived caches in one transaction. Keep only a content-free review-required marker if Francis ratifies it and the UI explicitly explains it; otherwise deletion leaves the state not assessed and must not unlock medical recommendations. Never retain a hash of deleted prose as a supposed privacy substitute. Deletion from the live database does not erase previous exported backups or guarantee forensic erasure of flash/WAL pages; describe that limitation accurately.

Backups must cover every first-slice table and scope relation, with a restore preview that makes inclusion of health notes explicit. The current envelope declares plaintext, not encryption (`packages/core-db/src/backup/contract.ts:39-59`). Before exporting new health information as a protected backup, WO-03 must supply reviewed encryption/password/recovery handling or Francis must explicitly choose and label a bounded unencrypted export policy. Do not silently omit health records and call the result a full backup. Restoring an older backup must not invent clearance; re-evaluate live prospective advice against restored unknown/review state.

## Exact proposed Migration 064 storage contract

All names below are **future proposed paths/entities**, not files or tables created here. Classifications apply to each listed field: **S** = safe-to-store now as non-executable user-reported data; **F** = needs Francis/integration decision on schema/product/privacy semantics; **C** = needs qualified clinical review before its specified executable meaning exists. S is not permission to merge SQL: the table shape still needs the shared-contract checkpoint. No C field belongs in the recommended first production 064.

### Shared identity and common laws

WO-05 supplies `activity_definition(activity_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY)`, `activity_series(series_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, activity_id TEXT NOT NULL)` and `activity_occurrence(occurrence_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, activity_id TEXT NOT NULL, series_id TEXT NULL)`. These key columns are **F**; WO-05 owns their complete schedule/content fields. This review does not invent a second activity schema. Neutral names/kind IDs are display taxonomy, not medical applicability. The proposed fifteen kinds and custom fallback remain inventory proposals (`docs/audits/accessible-coach/WO08_ACTIVITY_MOVEMENT_COVERAGE.md:87-109`). Applicability uses stable IDs, not names, fuzzy timestamps, kind-to-condition rules or strength movement IDs substituted for activities.

Recommend database-bound athlete ownership (**F**): no FK to `profile_slot`, because a saved training preset is not an athlete. A non-null adapter `athleteId` identifies the selected database in every API request/result; verify the registry/database binding before reads/writes. The integration owner must adopt the same convention for WO-05. All new IDs are opaque TEXT supplied by the adapter, distinct by entity, stable on rename/reschedule/restore. No ID or time generation in engines. Revision integers start at 1; times named `_at_ms` are non-negative safe-integer UTC epoch milliseconds. Date-only fields are ISO local calendar dates with an explicit IANA zone and no invented midnight.

All tables STRICT. All data writes parameterized, transactional and athlete-local. FKs and unique constraints must prevent orphan/cross-scope rows; all indexes, CHECKs and triggers are idempotent on self-heal. Migration 064 seeds no health records and infers no facts from existing `injury_flags`/`mobility_limits`. Legacy notes remain unchanged and visibly non-executable; a user may explicitly copy a note into a new record. Missing new tables after damage must cause a truthful unavailable/held recommendation state, not create an empty profile interpreted as clearance.

No function bodies are provided or implemented. `ContinueExistingGuards` means only that this support boundary has not held that scope; it is not a safety/clearance result and cannot bypass tier, equipment, access or existing halt laws. The evaluator must not accept note text as executable restriction input. The separate `SupportDisplayPayload` is never fed to pattern matching, embeddings or clinical decisions; `instructionRevisionFacts` contains IDs, lifecycle, explicit review requests and date-resolution state, not prose.

For the capture-only policy, explicit instruction-review holds and manual pause requests can return Held; the evaluator can never return “clinically cleared.” A future reviewed policy may add `EligibleWithinReviewedLimits` with exact policy/source/scope references, but that variant and its transitions are absent until clinical sign-off. A policy missing a metric, conflict rule, expiry interpretation or monitoring requirement returns Held rather than dropping it.

Evaluation is side-effect free; time, timezone resolution, registry/database identity, IDs, current connection status and policy versions are supplied. Sort reasons by a fixed reason-code order, then stable scope and instruction IDs/revisions; input ordering must not change output. Validate all revisions immediately before a transaction or work-state transition; on mismatch discard candidate advice and reevaluate. Persist an audit of the decisive IDs/revisions and reason codes without copying health prose into permanent session history. Hold changes do not update past session outcomes or dose snapshots.

No clinical composition is approved. A reported conflict, unknown applicability, unknown effective date/zone, expired instruction or unsupported unit cannot become an “unrestricted” empty list. A scheduled expiry triggers review at its explicit boundary; it never releases a hold automatically. A future confirmed compatible-limits rule might choose a stricter ceiling only with clinical approval; until then keep both sources and hold. Likewise a later instruction is not authority to supersede an earlier independent source merely because it was entered last.
