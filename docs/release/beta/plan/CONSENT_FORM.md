## What you are joining

You are invited because you are an adult already coached by the owner, with no current injury under treatment. About twelve athletes will try the Android app for fourteen days, with the owner as coach in Coach Mode. This is a test of logging, usability and the records needed to improve training decisions. Benefit is uncertain. It is not medical treatment or a test that proves the app is safe for everyone.

Follow your ordinary agreed training plan. Record the sets you actually do, answer the effort question honestly, record how the session felt and optionally add notes or training questions. You do not need to train more, lift a maximum or work through pain for this test. Exercise can cause soreness or injury; software can give an unsuitable suggestion, lose records or show an unhelpful answer. Contact the owner when something is wrong.

Stop on an app halt, injury or concerning symptoms and tell the owner the same day. Seek appropriate medical or urgent help when needed; do not wait for the app or coach to reply. The question box and session notes are not monitored emergency or coach messaging services. The owner will explain who to contact if unavailable.

## What you share

There is no automatic upload in this beta. You choose to hand the owner a password-protected encrypted backup in person, using USB/offline transfer, and give the password separately. Proposed schedule: a setup readability check, day 7 and day 14 or withdrawal. Ordinary contact with the coach for a safety concern is separate from log handover.

**The backup covers every athlete on your phone**, including profiles, identifiers, plans, workouts, activities, preferences and health-support records. It is not limited to this test or the active athlete. The app warns about this scope (`apps/mobile/src/components/BackupTransferPanel.tsx:35`). Do not include anyone else's records without resolving consent and isolation with the owner first. Records can include symptoms, health information, effort, notes, session feel and, when the flagged box is verified for this build, your typed questions and the card shown. Do not put someone else's personal information into notes or questions.

The owner and the named analyst above may read identifiable records only in the agreed private local environment, to compare planned and completed work, skips, loads shown and lifted, effort/repeatability, session feel and symptom/question responses. Raw files and identifiable text will not be uploaded to an outside analysis service. A clinical reviewer receives only separately agreed minimum de-identified information. The owner keeps the identity list separate and shares only anonymous aggregate findings outside that group.

Proposed handling: encrypted owner-controlled PC storage plus encrypted offline recovery storage; plaintext working copies are protected too. Proposed deletion is within 30 days of the owner's acceptance of the final analysis; the owner must enter the agreed calendar deletion date above and remove backups, working copies and transfer/recovery copies. Ask about any separate consent/incident record retention before signing. Encryption reduces exposure risk but cannot remove every risk of loss or unauthorized access.

## Your choice

Joining is voluntary. You can decline any optional question, ask questions, stop training or withdraw without affecting ordinary coaching. Tell the owner using the direct contact above. Withdrawal stops further collection; ask for removal of identifiable beta records still held. Already anonymous combined findings cannot be identified and removed.
